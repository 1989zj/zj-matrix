# 寿宴宫变篇 — Birthday Banquet Palace Coup Template

For chapters where a palace coup erupts **during** a high-stakes social event (贵妃/皇后寿宴). Unlike the 宫变序幕篇 (preparation before crisis), this chapter IS the crisis — the coup happens in real time, and the protagonist must navigate an actively collapsing situation.

## Triggering Signals

- The chapter is titled "血色寿宴" or similar banquet+coup combination
- Multiple power factions are present at a single formal gathering
- The protagonist enters armed (hidden weapon) knowing the event will go wrong
- The public setting means there are witnesses everywhere — reputation damage AND physical danger are both in play

## Structural Pattern (7 beats)

| # | Section | Purpose | Guideline length |
|---|---------|---------|-----------------|
| ① | 入宫·带刃 | Protagonist arrives at the palace, concealed weapon confirmed. Flash of前世死亡 memory at the same gate where she died. Goal: establish danger and trigger hatred | ~150 chars |
| ② | 入席·三方 | Banquet hall described. Three power factions seated: the host (贵妃/皇后), the accused (镇北王), the potential scapegoat (太子). Male lead and protagonist exchange one covert glance | ~200 chars |
| ③ | 发难 | Wine flows, music plays. Host suddenly claps hands — guards pour in. But they surround the ACCUSED faction, not the protagonist. **First twist**: the host is acting against her apparent ally | ~300 chars |
| ④ | 三方博弈 | Accused party calmly deflects blame to the scapegoat (太子). Three-way standoff — host, accused, scapegoat each threaten each other verbally without escalation. Protagonist realizes information she held (前世情报) is WRONG — the alliances are not what she thought | ~400 chars |
| ⑤ | 爆乱 | An explosion outside. Messengers rush in — there IS a coup happening, but from OUTSIDE the city walls (叛军攻城). The banquet-stalemate becomes irrelevant as everyone scrambles. **Second twist**: the external threat overrides the internal power struggle | ~350 chars |
| ⑥ | 男主密议 | In the chaos, male lead goes directly to the host. A quick whispered negotiation — protagonist can't hear the words, only sees the host's face shift through shock → disbelief → reluctant agreement | ~250 chars |
| ⑦ | 令牌·夜奔 | Male lead pulls protagonist into a side chamber. Brief, urgent exchange — he gives her a token and a mission. The token exploits her secret identity (金面绣娘/other) as the cover for leaving. She goes alone into the night. **Hook**: she's the only one who can get out | ~400 chars |

Total target: ~2050 chars skeleton; dress to 2100-2200.

## Style Rules

**Pacing**: Write FAST. Short paragraphs (1-3 sentences). Single-sentence lines for impact moments:

```
寂静。
死一般的寂静。
沈明薇愣住了。
```

**Three-power dialogue**: Each faction speaks in a distinct voice:
- Host (贵妃): warm surface → icy accusation → volatile fear
- Accused (镇北王): calm, slow, sardonic — paragraphs that deflect blame
- Scapegoat (太子): explosive fury → panicked denial → shocked silence

The protagonist should NOT speak during the three-way standoff. She watches and learns. Her silence distinguishes this pattern from 当众打脸篇 where she delivers the killing blow.

**The "情报错误" moment**: This is the emotional beat unique to this pattern — the protagonist discovers her前世 knowledge is wrong or incomplete. The alliances she thought she knew are different. This creates genuine uncertainty for both her and the reader, raising stakes beyond "she's prepared for everything."

**Cliffhanger**: The male lead sends her alone into danger. The final image is her back — she turns away from him into the dark. No promises, no declarations. The tension is in the separation.

## Key Differentiators from Similar Patterns

| Pattern | Crisis timing | Protagonist role | Romance beat |
|---------|--------------|-----------------|--------------|
| 入宫审问篇 | Pre-crisis (threat detected) | Defensive — evading interrogation | Male lead rescues + secret note |
| 宫变序幕篇 | Pre-crisis (preparation) | Co-conspirator — planning together | Hand touch + vulnerable share + 有一句话 hook |
| **寿宴宫变篇** (this) | **Mid-crisis (happening NOW)** | **Active — sent on a solo mission into danger** | **No romance — pure mission urgency. The trust IS the romance beat** |

## Overshoot Warning — HIGH DANGER

This pattern is the **second most overshoot-prone** (after 摊牌篇). Three factors inflate word count:

1. **Three-party dialogue**: Each speaker needs distinct framing, and the back-and-forth naturally fills space. A single confrontation exchange can run 400+ chars.
2. **Chaos description**: The explosion + messenger + crowd panic + individual reactions can balloon past 500 chars if you describe everyone.
3. **Witness reactions**: Unlike 摊牌篇 where only the elder's reaction matters, here EVERYBODY sees everything. Resist the urge to describe 5+ separate witness reactions.

**Real data**: A 2100-2200 target chapter written at 2480 chars (112%) — required 3 trim passes to reach 2214. Most of the bloat came from the crowd-panic scene (section ⑤) and over-describing the explosion/messenger arrival.

**Counter-strategy**: Write the skeleton first (the 7 beats stripped to dialogue + action, ~1600 chars). Then add ONE atmospheric layer per scene (not two or three). Check word count after every 200 chars of dressing. If you pass 1500 chars before section ③ (发难), you're already 100+ chars over and must trim the opening by 15%.

## Variants

- **Host-as-enemy**: Twist the 发难 — the host actually betrays her co-conspirators, but the protagonist doesn't trust her sincerity. The host's true allegiance remains ambiguous through the chapter.
- **Protagonist-as-target**: The guards surround the protagonist's family faction first, not the accused. She must talk her way out of being executed on the spot. The three-way standoff still occurs but she's an active participant, not an observer.
- **No external coup**: The explosion is a false alarm — the male lead's people staged it to create chaos as cover. In this variant, the令牌 mission is a decoy to get the protagonist to safety while he acts. She discovers the deception later.
- **Multiple cover identities**: The protagonist has MORE than one usable identity to leave. The choice of which identity to burn reveals character priority (金面绣娘 = safe but valuable reputation, vs an anonymous palace maid = fugitive but untraceable).
