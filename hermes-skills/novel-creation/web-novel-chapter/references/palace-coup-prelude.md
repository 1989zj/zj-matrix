# 宫变序幕篇 — Palace Coup Prelude Template

For chapters where the female and male leads prepare for an imminent palace coup / assassination / major crisis. The dramatic function is **establishing trust before the storm** — the couple coordinates their plan, the male lead reveals his vulnerable past, and the chapter ends with them about to face mortal danger together.

## Structural Pattern (7 beats)

| # | Section | Purpose | Guideline length |
|---|---------|---------|-----------------|
| ① | 闭门准备 | Protagonist makes covert preparations (swap guards/servants, feign illness) while maintaining a calm surface | ~150 chars |
| ② | 夜访密报 | Male lead sneaks into her quarters at night with urgent intelligence — scale and timing of the threat | ~250 chars |
| ③ | 主动请缨 | Male lead reveals he needs someone inside the fortress/palace. Protagonist volunteers without hesitation. He objects on safety grounds. | ~300 chars |
| ④ | 赠物定情 | Male lead gives her a family heirloom / protective token / weapon. Their hands touch. He doesn't pull away. Neither does she. | ~250 chars |
| ⑤ | 身世剖白 | Quiet aftermath. Male lead opens up about his past — mother's unjust death, childhood trauma, or the grudge that drives him. Protagonist realizes his frivolous exterior was armor. | ~350 chars |
| ⑥ | 临别许诺 | Departure at dawn. Male lead pauses at the door without turning around: "After this is over… there's something I want to tell you." Protagonist's heart races. | ~250 chars |
| ⑦ | 决战前夕 | Final one-liner time-jump to the event day. | ~100 chars |

Total target: ~1650 chars skeleton; dress to 2000-2100.

## Style Rules

**Tone**: First half tense and tactical (military details, surveillance reports, coded exchanges). Second half warm and intimate (moonlight conversation, childhood memories, unspoken feelings).

**Key romantic beat**: The 「指尖触碰」moment. His hand lingers on hers when he gives her the token. Keep it subtle — no declarations. The physical hesitation says everything.

**Key reveal rule**: The male lead's backstory must be **vulnerability he has never shown anyone else**. This is what transforms their relationship from alliance to emotional bond. Choose one specific, sensory memory from his childhood (e.g. his mother making him a lantern, the feel of kneeling on cold palace tiles).

**Cliffhanger**: The 「有一句话想对你说」hook is the critical element. The protagonist must NOT ask what it is, and he must NOT say it. The gap between "I have something to say" and "I'll say it after the crisis" is what powers reader anticipation.

## Overshoot Warning — MODERATE DANGER

This pattern has two wordy sections that inflate quickly:
- **Section ② (夜访密报)**: The intelligence report can balloon if you list too many tactical details (unit numbers, deployment plans, turncoat lists). Keep it to 1-2 concrete facts.
- **Section ⑤ (身世剖白)**: The male lead's backstory + protagonist's regret-laden inner thoughts can spiral. Limit protagonist's internal reaction to 1-2 sentences at most.

**Real data**: A 2000-target chapter written as first pass at 2962 chars (148%) — required two trim passes. The inflation came from over-writing the military briefing (section ②) and adding too much protagonist internal monologue during section ⑤.

## Variants

- **No token exchange**: If the male lead doesn't yet fully trust her, skip section ④ and move to section ⑤ (vulnerability precedes gift-giving). The chapter becomes bleaker but more realistic.
- **Group preparation**: If multiple allies are involved, expand section ② into a war-room scene. Split the intelligence report across multiple speakers.
- **Protagonist's heirloom**: Gender-flip section ④ — the protagonist gives the male lead a protective token instead. Works for strong-FMC stories where she has her own resources.

## Chapter Archetype Chart (where this fits)

| Chapter type | Setup → Crisis → Payoff? | Romance beat | Danger level |
|---|---|---|---|
| 宫变序幕篇 | All setup, zero payoff | Slow burn — hand touch + vulnerable share | High overshoot |
| 毒计察觉篇 | Scheme discovered, counter-prepared | Male lead delivers aid | Medium |
| 入宫审问篇 | Crisis faced and survived | Rescue + secret note | Low |
| 摊牌长辈篇 | Climax payoff against antagonist | Minimal | High overshoot |

The 宫变序幕篇 is the purest "calm before the storm" chapter. Its entire value is emotional — the reader knows the fighting starts next chapter. This chapter is for the characters to show who they are before the battle changes them.
