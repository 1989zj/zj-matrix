# Master Review Synthesis Template

Proven 7-section format for synthesizing 5 independent review reports into a single master review. Tested on two novels (末世污染等级评审 & 诡异游戏规则评审).

## Template Structure

```
# 《书名》—— 综合评审报告 (MASTER)

**评审范围**：第1~N章（约X万字，连载状态）
**评审类型**：类型标签
**评审日期**：YYYY-MM-DD
**综合评分：XX / 100（B+级）**

---
```

## Score Computation

Composite score = average of 5 dimension scores:
```
(Structure + Characters + Writing + Compliance + Market) / 5
```

Where each dimension is on a 100-point scale. For the market/commercial dimension, convert from 10-point to 100-point (multiply by 10).

## Key Principles

1. **Evidence first** — every claim about strengths or weaknesses must cite a specific chapter and ideally a line range. Never make assertions without evidence.
2. **Severity ordering** — issues MUST be listed in descending severity (🔴→🟡→🟢), not grouped by topic or chapter range.
3. **Commercial realism** — word count viability is always the #1 or #2 commercial constraint. < 60万字 = structural limitation.
4. **P0 ≠ hardest** — P0 means "most urgent for reader retention/publishing timeline," not technically hardest.
5. **One-line verdict** — the last line of the report must be a single sentence the user can paste as a summary to others.
6. **No binary score inflation** — compliance tends to score 90-100 (safe novels pass easily), while market/structure tend 65-80. This is normal. Don't artificially inflate lower scores.
